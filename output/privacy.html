Privacy Policy

How Medicare Monitor handles data, cookies, and advertising.

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Who we are

Medicare Monitor ("we", "us") is an independent news aggregation service that collects headlines and short excerpts from third-party publishers and links readers to the original articles. We are not affiliated with the U.S. government, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), or the official Medicare program.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to read this site. Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) automatically processes standard technical data — such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested — to deliver the site securely and measure aggregate traffic. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

Advertising and cookies

We display advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message before any personalized advertising cookies are set, and you can change your choice at any time.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate or sponsored links (for example, links to Medicare plan comparison services). If you click one and take an action, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Third-party content and links

All news content on this site links to the original publisher. Once you follow a link off this site, the destination publisher's own privacy policy applies. We display only headlines, sources, publication dates, and short excerpts; we do not reproduce full articles.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its effective date, will always be published at this address.
